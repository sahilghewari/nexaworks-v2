## The speed gap we kept seeing

Every founder we spoke to had the same story: six-month timelines, endless steering committees, and a demo that never survived first contact with production data. Big consultancies are optimized for predictable revenue, not for getting your product live. That tension shows up as layered approvals, generic playbooks, and teams that rotate every sprint.

## Incentives work against speed

Enterprise vendors win with utilization and headcount, not outcomes. Project scopes grow to justify bodies. Roadmaps become 200-page decks. Engineers are swapped mid-flight to balance utilization. The result is slower delivery, weaker context, and brittle handoffs.

## Process tax and tool bloat

Most large vendors mandate heavy SDLC checkpoints. Each gate adds calendar weeks. Add in proprietary tooling that no one asked for and you lose the ability to ship quickly. When we built NexaWorks we cut to three rituals: weekly demo, daily unblock, Friday risk review. Everything else is optional.

## Risk avoidance versus risk ownership

Risk avoidance means delaying decisions until every stakeholder signs off. Risk ownership means shipping a small version now, measuring, and course-correcting in public. At NexaWorks we prefer the latter. It forces clarity, exposes integration issues early, and keeps everyone aligned on measurable outcomes.

## A real timeline comparison

- Traditional: 4-6 weeks for discovery decks, 8-12 weeks for an MVP that rarely hits production, and another quarter for stabilization.
- NexaWorks: 1 week to align on KPIs, 3-4 weeks to ship a demo on real data, 2 weeks to harden and integrate, then iterate with telemetry.

## The NexaWorks approach

1. Founders who code stay on the hook for every sprint.
2. Scope only what we can demo within two weeks.
3. Observability by default, so decisions are data-backed.
4. Transparent risks every Friday.

## What this means for you

If you are tired of slow cycles, insist on teams that lead with working software and measurable KPIs. Ask for the timeline above. If you do not see a live demo in week three, change the team.
