## The Inbox is a Fortress

If you are a founder or a VP of Sales, you’ve seen the numbers. Open rates are down, reply rates are abysmal, and "I'm interested" responses feel like winning the lottery. 

The traditional SDR model—hiring three juniors to blast 100 emails a day each—is officially dead. In 2026, Google and Microsoft’s AI-powered spam filters can spot a "standard" template in milliseconds. If your email looks like a sequence, it’s going to the Promotions tab or, worse, the trash.

## Why Volume is a Self-Inflicted Wound

When you lead with volume, you are signaling to the market that you don't know who they are. 
1. **Brand Damage**: Founders talk. If you spam a Series B founder with a generic pitch, they remember it.
2. **Burned Domains**: High volume + low engagement = blacklisted domains. 
3. **Burned SDRs**: Nobody wants to spend 8 hours a day being ignored. High turnover is a hidden tax on your growth.
