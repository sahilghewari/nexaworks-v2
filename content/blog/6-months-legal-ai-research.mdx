## Why we did the research

Legal workflows in India are fragmented across courts, cities, and practice areas. Precedent search is slow and largely manual. We wanted to understand if AI could help without breaking trust or compliance.

## How we structured the six months

- 300 interviews across litigators, in-house counsel, and legal ops teams
- Shadowed hearings to map real timelines and information flows
- Prototyped copilots for drafting, precedent search, and case clustering
- Ran compliance and privacy reviews with partner firms

## Patterns that emerged

1. Lawyers trust sources, not black boxes. Citations and traceability are mandatory.
2. Drafting speed matters, but risk controls matter more.
3. Collaboration is fractured: WhatsApp threads, PDFs, and email all at once.

## Early prototypes

We built a knowledge graph linking statutes, orders, and briefs. We added retrieval-augmented generation so every suggestion showed its source. A clustering view grouped similar cases to speed up research.

## Outcomes so far

- 300 lawyer interviews distilled into a feature roadmap
- Prototypes that lawyers could test on their own matters
- A pilot plan that sequences drafting, research, and matter management

## What is next for NyayMind

We are aligning with a pilot cohort, hardening data pipelines, and formalizing compliance checks. If you are building in legal or want to shape the roadmap, we would love to collaborate.
