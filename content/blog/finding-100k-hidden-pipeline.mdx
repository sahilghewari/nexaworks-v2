## The "Leaky Bucket" Problem

We’ve audited over 50 B2B funnels this year. In almost every case, the founder thought they had a "Lead Gen" problem. In reality, they had a **Pipeline Architecture** problem.

If you are spending money on ads or SDRs but your "SQL to Close" rate is flat, you are pouring water into a leaky bucket.

## Our 14-Day Audit Checklist

When we step in for a **Strategy & Pipeline Audit**, we don't look at "likes" or "clicks." We look at revenue. Here is the 4-step process we use to find at least $100k in hidden opportunities:

### 1. ICP Laser-Focusing
Most teams target "SaaS Founders." That's too broad. We find the **sub-segment** that is currently experiencing a "Trigger Event." 
* *Example:* "Fintech companies that just raised a Series A and are hiring their first 5 engineers."
* **The Opportunity:** A hyper-niche ICP usually doubles conversion rates instantly.

### 2. The Offer Teardown
Is your offer "We build software" or is it "We reduce your cloud spend by 30% in 90 days or you don't pay"? 
If your offer isn't an "Irresistible Bribe," no amount of AI outreach will save you.

### 3. Funnel Velocity Audit
We track exactly where prospects drop off. 
* Is the "Book a Demo" page too slow? 
* Is the "Follow-up" taking 24 hours instead of 5 minutes? 
* **The Opportunity:** Automating the first 5 minutes of lead response often increases close rates by 40%.

### 4. Technical Infrastructure (The Engine Room)
We check your deliverability. Are 30% of your emails landing in spam without you knowing? Are your LinkedIn profiles optimized for conversion or do they look like resumes?
